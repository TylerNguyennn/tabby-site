How to save tabs for later and close them in Chrome

Save any tab to Later, close it, and open it again any time. A green check shows which open tabs are already saved.

Step by step

What you’ll do

Save any open tab to Later, close it, and open it again whenever you want.

Find your open tabs

  1. Open a new tab to open Tabby’s Home.
  2. Under Open tabs, hover over a tab and click the save icon.
  3. In the Save to menu, click Later, the list of pages to read another time.

Close it

  1. When Tabby says Saved to “Later”, click Close the tab in the message; the tab closes but stays saved.
  2. To save a tab and leave it open, click its save icon, click Later, and skip the message.
  3. A green check next to an open tab means it is already saved and safe to close any time.

Open it again

  1. In the sidebar, click Later.
  2. Click the saved page to open it again.

Shortcut

  1. On any page, press ⌥⇧S / Alt+Shift+S to open Tabby’s save box with Later already picked.
  2. Press Enter to save the tab to Later.
  3. To close tabs right after saving, turn on Close tabs after saving in Settings.

Good to know

  • Prefer to close right after saving? Settings → "Close tabs after saving".
  • Save to a collection instead of Later: pick it in the same "Save to" menu.
