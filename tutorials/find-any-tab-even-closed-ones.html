How to find an open or recently closed tab in Chrome

One search box for your open tabs, saved pages and tabs you closed in the last 14 days. Press Command K (Ctrl K on Windows).

Step by step

What you’ll do

Search your open tabs, saved pages and recently closed tabs from one search box, then open any result.

Open the search

  1. Open a new tab and press ⌘K / Ctrl+K.
  2. A search box opens with a few actions in it; start typing.
  3. On Tabby’s pages, pressing / also opens the search when you’re not typing in a box.

Type a word

  1. Type a word from the page, such as invoice.
  2. Results come in groups: Saved links, Open tabs and Closed earlier.
  3. Tabs you closed in the last 14 days show under Closed earlier, with when you closed them.

Open it

  1. Press the down arrow until the result is highlighted.
  2. Press Enter; a closed tab opens again, and an open tab is brought to the front.

Good to know

  • Press / to search when you're not typing in a box.
  • Type a command too, like "new collection".
