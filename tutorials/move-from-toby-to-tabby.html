How to move from Toby to Tabby and keep your collections

Export your Toby collections to a file, then import it in Tabby’s Settings: 5 collections and 24 links in about a minute.

Step by step

What you’ll do

Export your Toby collections to a file, then import that file so every Toby list becomes a Tabby collection.

Export from Toby

  1. In Toby, click your name at the top left.
  2. Click Export; Toby saves a .json file to your Downloads folder.

Import into Tabby

  1. Open a new tab and, at the bottom of Tabby’s sidebar, click Settings.
  2. Scroll down to Your data.
  3. In the Import section, click Choose file…, pick the Toby file in Downloads, and click Open.
  4. Check what Tabby found (in the video, 5 collections and 24 links) and click Import.

Your new space

  1. Your Toby lists are now collections in a new space called Imported from Toby.
  2. Links Tabby can’t open, such as chrome:// pages, are skipped.

Good to know

  • Tabby reads Toby’s .json export; links Tabby can’t open (like chrome:// pages) are skipped.
