How to close duplicate tabs in Chrome

Tabby spots pages that are open more than once. One click closes the extra copies and keeps the newest; Undo brings them back.

Step by step

What you’ll do

Close every extra copy of pages you have open more than once, in one click.

Close the copies

  1. Open a new tab and look under Open tabs on Home.
  2. A mark such as 3× next to a tab means that page is open three times.
  3. A line in Open tabs says how many pages are open more than once.
  4. At the end of that line, click Close the copies.
  5. Tabby closes the extra copies and keeps the newest copy of each page open.

Recap

  1. To bring the copies back, click Undo in the message.
  2. Close the copies is also in the side panel and in Home’s ⋯ menu.
