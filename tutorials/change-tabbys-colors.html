How to change Tabby’s colors and turn on dark mode

29 themes, with fonts and icons to match. Graphite, Catppuccin, Dracula, Zen Garden, Sakura and Capy Chill are free; the other 23 come with Tabby Pro.

1:13 · English voice, English and Vietnamese captions (CC) · Watch on YouTube (opens YouTube) ↗

Step by step

  1. In the sidebar, at the bottom, click Settings.
  2. Under Appearance, find Theme and click Browse themes to see all 29.
  3. Under Mode, click Dark, or click Match system to follow your computer.
  4. Click a theme card, such as Dracula.
  5. Graphite, Catppuccin, Dracula, Zen Garden, Sakura and Capy Chill are free; themes marked Pro come with Tabby Pro.
  6. Click the X at the top (Close settings) to see Home in the new colors.

Good to know

  • The video shows Tabby 1.1, which had eight themes in Settings. Since 1.2 there are 29, and they open from Browse themes.
  • The other 23 themes (such as Tokyo Night, Rosé Pine, Gruvbox, Neon Alley and Arcade) come with Tabby Pro, with a 14-day free trial, no card needed. When it ends, Tabby goes back to Graphite and remembers your choice.
