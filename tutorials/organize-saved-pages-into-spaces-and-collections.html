How to organize saved tabs by project in Chrome

Keep each project in its own space, with collections inside: create them, drag tabs in, and give them a color.

Step by step

What you’ll do

  1. Keep each project in its own space, with collections inside that hold its saved pages.
  2. A space is like a big folder for one project, such as Work; a collection is a smaller list inside it, such as Work sites.

Make a space

  1. In the sidebar, click New space.
  2. Type a name such as Website redesign and press Enter.

Add a collection

  1. On the space page, click New collection.
  2. Type a name such as Docs and press Enter.

Save a tab into it

  1. Find the tab in the open tabs list on the right of the space page.
  2. Drag the tab onto the collection and let go; the page is now saved there.
  3. Without a mouse, use a saved page’s ⋯ menu → Move to… to move it.

Give it a color

  1. On the collection, click its three dots (⋯) and click Color & icon.
  2. Pick a color, such as blue; the collection now shows a colored mark.
  3. Color & icon also offers icons to tell collections apart.

Good to know

  • No mouse? ⋯ → Move to… moves a page without dragging.
  • Color & icon also has icons (📚, 💼, ✈️…) to tell collections apart.
