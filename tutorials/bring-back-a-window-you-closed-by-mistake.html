How to reopen a window you closed by mistake in Chrome

Closed a window by mistake? Tabby offers it back in one click. Older windows stay in History for 14 days.

Step by step

What you’ll do

Get back every tab from a window you closed by mistake, in one click.

Bring it back

  1. Open a new tab; at the top of Tabby’s Home, a banner says a window was closed and how many tabs it had.
  2. On the banner, click Bring it back; the window opens again with all its tabs.

Older windows

  1. In the sidebar, click History; closed windows are kept there for 14 days.
  2. Click a closed window to see its tabs.
  3. To keep its tabs for later, click Save as collection.
  4. To open the window now, click Restore.
  5. History also lists single closed tabs and the Trash.

Good to know

  • History also lists single closed tabs and the Trash.
