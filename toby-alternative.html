Why people switch from Toby

The 60-tab limit. Toby's free Starter plan saves up to 60 tabs. Over the limit you can still open and export them, but you can't save new ones until you delete some or upgrade. Tabby has no cap on saved tabs, collections or spaces, free.

Privacy. Tabby has no account and no analytics. Your tabs and links stay in your browser and are never sent to anyone (privacy policy).

More than saving. Tabby also cleans up what you have open: Tidy up goes through tabs one at a time (keep, close or save with one key), Close the copies removes duplicate tabs, Sleep frees memory, and History brings back a window you closed by mistake.

When Toby is the better choice

If you need your collections on several computers or your phone, or you share collections with a team, Toby does that and Tabby doesn't (yet). Tabby is for one person on one browser who wants it fast, private and free of limits.

How to move from Toby to Tabby

  1. In Toby, export your collections as a .json file.
  2. Install Tabby and open a new tab.
  3. On the welcome screen choose Coming from Toby, OneTab or bookmarks? (or later: Settings → Your data → Import) and pick the file. Your collections and links come over with their names.

Questions

Is there a free Toby alternative without the 60-tab limit?

Yes. Tabby is free with unlimited saved tabs, collections and spaces, and no account. It runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox.

Can I import my Toby collections into Tabby?

Yes. Export your Toby data as a .json file and import it on Tabby's welcome screen or in Settings → Your data. Collections keep their names and links.

Does Tabby sync between computers like Toby?

Not yet. Tabby keeps everything on your device for privacy. You can export a backup, or turn on automatic daily or weekly backups, and import it on another computer.
